# Tessera: pricing

| Plan       | Price                                   | Users           |
|------------|-----------------------------------------|-----------------|
| Free       | $0                                      | up to 3 users   |
| Team       | $10 per user per month, billed monthly  | 4 to 100 users  |
| Enterprise | custom                                  | over 100 users  |

- Annual billing on the Team plan gives two months free.
- Version history is kept for 30 days on Free and Team, and for 365 days on Enterprise.
- Email support answers within one business day on all paid plans.
