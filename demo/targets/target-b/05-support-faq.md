# Tessera: support FAQ

**How many devices can I sync?** Up to 5 devices per user, on every plan.

**How long are old versions kept?** 90 days on the Team plan.

**Where is my data stored?** In the EU, in Frankfurt.

**How fast does support reply?** Within one business day for paid plans. The Free plan has community support only.

**Can I work offline?** Yes. Changes sync when the device is back online.
