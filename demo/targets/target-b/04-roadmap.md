# Tessera: roadmap

Tessera 1.0 shipped in January 2025. Version 2.4 is the current release.

- Version 2.5, planned for the fourth quarter of 2026, adds a Linux client.
- Version 3.0, planned for the second quarter of 2027, adds end-to-end encryption. Until then files are encrypted in transit and at rest only.
- An Android app is planned. An iOS app is not planned.
- The Windows and macOS clients are available today.
